Spooky Halloween Trivia & World Records

🍬 Massive Candy Sales

Over 600 million pounds of candy are bought in the United States alone every Halloween season—with chocolate taking up over 75% of total sales!

🌕 Rare Full Moons

A full moon on Halloween night is rare! It only occurs once every 18 to 19 years. The last Halloween full moon occurred in 2020, and the next will happen in 2039.

🎃 Heavyweight Pumpkin Record

The heaviest Jack-o'-Lantern pumpkin ever grown weighed an astonishing 2,749 pounds (1,247 kg)—heavier than a compact sports car!

🧛 Samhainophobia

Did you know there is a formal psychological name for an intense fear of Halloween? It is officially called Samhainophobia!

🌽 Candy Corn History

Invented in the 1880s by George Renninger, Candy Corn was originally manufactured under the commercial product name "Chicken Feed" because corn was considered animal food!

🔮 Illinois Pumpkin Capital

The state of Illinois produces over 80% of all processed canned pumpkins in the United States, making it the pumpkin capital of the world.
