How the World Celebrates Autumn & Spirits

Mexico

Día de los Muertos

Celebrated Nov 1–2, "Day of the Dead" is a vibrant celebration honoring deceased ancestors with marigold flowers, sugar skulls, pan de muerto bread, and home altars (ofrendas) loaded with favorite treats.

Ireland

Púca Festival

Ireland honors Halloween's original Celtic roots in County Meath with torchlight processions, traditional fires, Gaelic storytelling, music, and festive Barmbrack fruit bread with hidden fortune coins.

Japan

Kawasaki Parade

In Japan, Halloween focuses on high-end adult cosplay culture. Tens of thousands gather in massive street parades showcasing incredible anime, horror, and pop-culture costumes.

China

Teng Chieh Festival

Bonfires and lanterns are lit across China to guide ancestral spirits back home on Halloween night, while food and water bowls are set before photographs of departed loved ones.

Austria

Seleenwoche (All Souls)

In Austria, people leave bread, water, and a lit lamp on the table before sleeping during All Souls' Week to welcome dead souls back to their family home for the night.

Haiti

Fed Gede Rituals

Vodou traditions in Haiti celebrate Fed Gede on November 1–2 with drums, dancing, white face powder, and street processions honoring ancestral spirits led by Baron Samedi.
